HOW TO USE IT RIGHT NOW
================================================================================

Step 1 (30 seconds):
  from dating_agent.profile_aggregator import profile_aggregator
  agg = profile_aggregator()

Step 2 (1 minute):
  auth = agg.hinge_auth
  req = auth.request_phone_verification("+1-555-123-4567")
  verify = auth.verify_phone_otp(req['otp_id'], "123456")
  user_id = verify['user_id']

Step 3 (2-5 minutes):
  result = agg.run_full_sync(user_id)

Step 4 (instant):
  agg.export_sync_report("results.json")

DONE. Your profiles are synced, stored, and reported.
